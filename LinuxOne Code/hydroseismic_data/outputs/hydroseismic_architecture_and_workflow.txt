HYDROSEISMIC
Earthquake and Flood Incident Monitoring Prototype
==================================================

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Public earthquake and flood incident information is published by
different sources and in different formats. HydroSeismic combines
selected public incident records into a common dataset for exploration
and transparent prototype prioritization.

2. DATA SOURCES
- USGS: earthquake event feed.
- GDACS: earthquake and flood event information, including official
  alert levels where available.

5. PRIORITY CALCULATION
The prototype combines earthquake magnitude points with points based
on the GDACS official alert level when available.

Magnitude contribution:
- Earthquake magnitude is capped at 7 for this calculation.
- Magnitude points increase nonlinearly with magnitude.
- The maximum magnitude contribution is 70 points.

GDACS alert contribution:
- Red: 30 points
- Orange: 20 points
- Green: 10 points
- Missing alert: 0 points

The total is capped at 100.

Calculated priority bands:
- Low: 0 to below 30
- Medium: 30 to below 60
- High: 60 to 100

Current distribution:
- High: 5
- Medium: 99
- Low: 184

6. IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
Calculated priority is an experimental heuristic for organizing
incidents. It is not an official warning, a validated risk estimate,
or a disaster prediction.

GDACS official alert levels and HydroSeismic calculated priority are
different fields and must not be presented as equivalent.

The prototype does not establish that the score predicts damage,
casualties, or future events.
